# जुवा — कौडा (Juwa: Kauda)

A Nepali Tihar-style cowrie-shell (कौडा) betting game for mobile, played with **fake credits only**: no real money.

**Play:** open `index.html` in a phone browser. It is a single file. Credits and stats are saved in the browser.

## How it plays
- The banker bot **साहुजी** throws 16 cowries. Mouth-up = **चित**, back-up = **पट**.
- Bet on how many land चित:
  - कम (0–7) ×2.4
  - बढी (9–16) ×2.4
  - जोर (even) ×1.9
  - बिजोर (odd) ×1.9
  - an exact number (×4.5 for 8, up to huge payouts for 0 or 16)
- Multipliers are the total return, including your stake.
- **काले दाइ** is a rival bot player who bets every round.
- Empty साहुजी's purse (10,000) to bankrupt him.
- Out of credits? **+ क्रेडिट** adds 100 / 500 / 1000 free credits. The credits screen also shows stats and a full reset.
